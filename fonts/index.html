NMBS MS96 interior dotmatrix font

Note: the characters "I", "J", "K", "Q", "R", "X", "Y", "q" and "y" are drawn based on guesses, as well as every digit as these are not shown on these displays normally. To type the NMBS logo, use the character "|". For the punctuation marks, "-", ":", "'", ".", "/" are realistic, the other punctuation marks have also been guessed. All other characters are pixel perfect recreations. Special thanks to rle200428 for providing reference material.

SGMm dotmatrix font

This is a recreation of the font used on both the exterior and interior dotmatrix screens of Dutch NS SGMm (Sprinter) trains.

Note: the characters "q", "F", "I", "P", "T" and "!" were drawn as guesses based on the other characters.

Note: the characters "G", "J", "K", "Q", "W", "X", "Y", "f", "x", "y", "z", "0", "2", "3", "7" and "9" are currently unfortunately missing due to a lack of reference material. Special thanks to dino.0412_ and Webguy for helping with getting reference material.

Arriva Limburg Stadler GTW-D Flipdot font

This is a pixel perfect recreation of the font used on the exterior front destination flipdot displays of Arriva Stadler GTW-D trains, specifically those that operate on the Maaslijn. Special thanks to dino.0412_ for prodiving a lot of picture reference material for these letters.

Note: not all capital letters (and no special characters) are supported due to a lack of reference material
